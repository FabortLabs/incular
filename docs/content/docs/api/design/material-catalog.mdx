The Material catalog ports the Flutter Material components that most desktop
applications reach for, in the Material 3 metrics. Every item below is in
`incular::material_prelude`.

```rust
use incular::prelude::*;
use incular::material_prelude::*;

fn main() {
    let app = Application::new(|_cx| {
        let table = DataTable::new(
            [
                DataColumn::text("Name").on_sort(|_column, _ascending| {}),
                DataColumn::text("Age").numeric(true),
            ],
            [
                DataRow::new([DataCell::text("Ada"), DataCell::text("36")])
                    .on_select_changed(|_selected| {}),
                DataRow::new([DataCell::text("Linus"), DataCell::text("54")]).selected(true),
            ],
        )
        .sort_column_index(Some(0));
        let view = SegmentedButton::new(
            [
                ButtonSegment::new(1).text("Day"),
                ButtonSegment::new(2).text("Week"),
                ButtonSegment::new(3).text("Month"),
            ],
            [2],
        )
        .on_selection_changed(|selection| println!("{selection:?}"));
        let steps = Stepper::new([
            Step::new(Text::new("Account"), Text::new("Details")).is_active(true),
            Step::new(Text::new("Confirm"), Text::new("Done")).state(StepState::Complete),
        ]);
        let more = ExpansionTile::text("Advanced").children([Text::new("Hidden options")]);
        MaterialApp::new(Scaffold::new(Column::new(Vec::<Widget>::from([
            view.into(),
            table.into(),
            steps.into(),
            more.into(),
        ]))))
        .into()
    })
    .expect("valid application");
    incular::run(app).expect("run application");
}
```

## Data

- `DataTable` lays out `DataColumn`s and `DataRow`s of `DataCell`s with
  Flutter's metrics: 56 px heading, 48 px rows, a 24 px margin and 56 px
  column spacing. It supports sort arrows, selection checkboxes and numeric
  columns.
- `PaginatedDataTable` reads rows from a `DataTableSource` and shows them a
  page at a time inside a `Card`. It has a header, a footer, `page_to` and
  `on_page_changed`.

## Pickers

- `CalendarDatePicker` shows a month grid and a year grid. It supports
  `selectable_day_predicate`, `select`, `show_month` and `displayed_month`.
- `DatePickerDialog`, `DateRangePickerDialog` and `TimePickerDialog` (a dial
  that supports `always_use_24_hour_format`) are the dialog forms.
- `show_date_picker`, `show_date_range_picker` and `show_time_picker` open
  the dialogs and return a `DialogResultHandle` with the chosen `Date`,
  `DateTimeRange` or `TimeOfDay`.
- `Date` is a proleptic Gregorian date with `weekday`, `add_days`,
  `add_months` and formatting helpers.

## Search and selection

- `SearchBar` is a standalone search field.
- `SearchAnchor` opens a suggestions view from an anchor.
  `SearchAnchor::bar` builds one around a `SearchBar`, and
  `SearchController` opens, closes and fills it. `search_suggestion` builds
  a list tile for a suggestion.
- `SegmentedButton` takes `ButtonSegment`s, with single or multiple
  selection (`multi_selection_enabled`) and optional empty selection.
  `ToggleButtons` is the older toggle row.

## Structure

- `Stepper` shows `Step`s vertically or horizontally (`StepperType`).
- `ExpansionTile` is driven by an optional `ExpansionTileController`.
  `ExpansionPanelList` shows a list of `ExpansionPanel`s.
- `CarouselView` is a horizontally scrolling item strip with a
  `CarouselController`.
- `ReorderableListView` has drag handles on desktop and long-press dragging
  elsewhere.
- `GridTile` and `GridTileBar` show a tile with a header or footer bar.
- `TabPageSelector` shows dots for a `TabController`.
- `FlexibleSpaceBar` is the collapsing area of a `SliverAppBar`, with
  parallax or pinned collapse.

## Surfaces and feedback

- `MaterialBanner` shows a message with actions.
- `show_modal_bottom_sheet` and `ModalBottomSheet` show a modal sheet with a
  drag handle, at most 640 px wide.
- `RefreshIndicator` is pull-to-refresh driven by scroll notifications; a
  `RefreshCompleter` ends the refresh.
- `AboutDialog`, `AboutListTile` and `LicensePage` show the licenses
  registered with `LicenseRegistry`.
- `AnimatedIcon` shows `AnimatedIcons` at a progress.
- `InkRipple`, `InkSplash`, `InkSparkle` and `NoSplash` are the splash
  factories.

## Known differences from Flutter

- `ExpansionTile` expands and collapses at once instead of animating its
  size.
- The `SearchAnchor` view opens below the anchor instead of covering it.
- The time picker dial has no hand.
- `AnimatedIcon` cross-fades two glyphs instead of morphing paths.
- `CarouselView` items do not shrink at the edges.
